Universal Dependencies treebanks, release 2.19 (https://universaldependencies.org/)

The correction lists in training/overrides/ and the sentence starters in
simplemma/sentences.py (cs, de, fr, nl, pt) are mined from the train splits
listed below, under their own licenses. Only CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0 and
public-domain treebanks are used, except for Ancient Greek (grc) and Latin
(la), where CC BY-NC-SA
treebanks are also used. Data derived from them is restricted to
non-commercial use under the terms of that license. For commercial use,
delete grc.plzma and la.plzma from simplemma/strategies/dictionaries/data/,
which removes both languages.

Rows whose pair none of these treebanks contains are attested by Wiktionary
(Kaikki) or were reviewed by hand.
